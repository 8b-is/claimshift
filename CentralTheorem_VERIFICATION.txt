Classical.choice is confined to the forward direction (and the iff,
which inherits it from the forward direction): it selects one common
admissible action per observation class. In the operational regime
(finite worlds, finite action vocabulary) that selection is a
decidable finite search, and the engine implements it constructively
(crates/admissibility::guaranteeing_policy in 8b-is-engine). The
backward direction, the No-Safe-Action corollary, and the
minimal-history projection are fully constructive and carry no
axioms at all.

propext appears in exactly one non-vacuity witness
(no_safe_action_witness_example), through the simp/iff machinery that
unfolds the definitional `if`-based admissibility predicate on Bool
worlds. propext is one of Lean 4's two standard core axioms
(propositional extensionality); it is neither Classical.choice nor
sorryAx. The specimen's headline theorems likewise record propext in
their footprints, so this is within the established discipline. No
theorem in this file depends on sorryAx; no hidden sorry anywhere in
the dependency chain of any headline theorem.

Declared dependency graph, as designed:

  epistemic_overlap_forward, epistemic_overlap_backward
    -> epistemic_overlap_iff

  no_safe_action (CT-000+) is a direct constructive refutation
    of a guaranteeing policy from a blurred, admissibility-relevant
    observation class; it is not downstream of the iff.

  ct015_prefix_projection (CT-015) is an independent factorization

  The two witness examples are independent constructive existentials

    downstream of the headline theorems.
